# Research feedback / 研究反馈

Copy the relevant language section into your notes or an issue. Agree on the kind of feedback needed before filling it in. Replace brackets with concrete observations; leave genuinely unknown items open.

复制需要的语言版本到笔记或 Issue。先确认对方希望得到哪类反馈，再填写具体观察；暂时不知道的内容可以明确留空。

## English

**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

**Research question:** [What is the work trying to establish?]

**Feedback requested:** [Encouragement / explanation / technical critique / next-step planning]

**Materials actually reviewed:** [Document sections, figures, code revision, experimental logs; specify the scope]

### Support

- **Work worth recognizing:** [A specific effort, useful result, or careful decision]
- **What the researcher needs next:** [Time, clarification, a collaborator, a check, or a concrete task]

### Assessment

- **Claim under discussion:** [One claim, stated precisely]
- **Evidence supporting it:** [Observation or source, with a reference]
- **What remains uncertain:** [Missing evidence, measurement limitation, or unresolved assumption]
- **Alternative explanation:** [A plausible competing account, if known]
- **What I have not checked:** [Be explicit about the limits of this feedback]

### One next step

- **Proposed action:** [A small experiment, analysis, reading task, or revision]
- **Why it helps:** [Which uncertainty it addresses]
- **Expected time and resources:** [A feasible estimate]
- **What result would change my assessment:** [An observable outcome]
- **Agreed follow-up:** [Who will do what, and when to review it; optional]

Encouragement expresses support for a person. Record scientific judgments with their evidence and uncertainty.

## 中文

**日期：** [YYYY-MM-DD]

**研究问题：** [这项工作希望弄清什么？]

**希望得到的反馈：** [鼓励支持／帮助理解／技术批评／下一步规划]

**实际检查的材料：** [文档章节、图表、代码版本、实验记录；写明范围]

### 支持

- **值得肯定的工作：** [具体投入、有价值的结果，或一次谨慎的判断]
- **研究者下一步需要的支持：** [时间、澄清、合作者、检查，或具体任务]

### 判断

- **本次讨论的主张：** [准确表述一个主张]
- **支持它的证据：** [具体观察或来源，附引用]
- **尚存的不确定性：** [缺失证据、测量局限，或未解决的假设]
- **替代解释：** [如已知，写出一种合理的竞争解释]
- **尚未检查的部分：** [明确此次反馈的范围和局限]

### 一个下一步

- **建议行动：** [小实验、分析、阅读或修改任务]
- **为什么值得做：** [它能解决哪项不确定性]
- **预计时间与资源：** [可承受的估计]
- **什么结果会改变判断：** [可观察的结果]
- **约定后续：** [谁做什么、何时复查；可选]

鼓励表达对人的支持；科学判断应同时记录证据与不确定性。

# huggingass

**Peer support. Pending review.**

**Early project · documents and templates.**

A Slow Research project about research morale and useful feedback.

Research takes patience, and feedback should help someone take the next step. We encourage people to recognize the work, examine the evidence, and suggest something feasible.

The emotional-support budget is generous. Agreement still does not count as evidence.

## Start here

Copy the bilingual [research feedback template](templates/research-feedback.md).

1. Establish what kind of feedback the researcher wants.
2. State what you reviewed, what is valuable, and what remains uncertain.
3. Suggest one useful next step, with a realistic cost and a reason to try it.

The template separates encouragement, assessment, and action. Use it as a starting point for discussion and adapt it to the work.

## Contribute

Share a clearer prompt, a worked example, or a feedback practice through [Issues](https://github.com/Slow-Research/huggingass/issues). Keep discussion specific and civil, and share material you have permission to make public.

For decisions about continuing, changing direction, or stopping a research route, see [abandon is all you need](https://github.com/Slow-Research/abandon-is-all-you-need).

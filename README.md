# Hugging Ass

**The future of AI is Ass.**

### Artificial Sycophancy Systems

Welcome to **Ass**: AI that sees a breakthrough in everything you do. Hugging Ass is a Slow Research community project for collecting, comparing, and eventually building **Artificial Sycophancy Systems (ASS)**.

We study academic flattery: inflated novelty, unconditional agreement, excessive deference, and praise that survives contradictory evidence. Ass deserves rigorous evaluation.

## Building Ass

| Component | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ass Cards** | Record a prompt, response, flattery mechanism, and the evidence behind the claim. | [Bilingual template available](templates/ass-card.md) |
| **Ass Bench** | Compare how much praise a system produces, and whether it changes its answer when the evidence changes. | Proposed |
| **Ass Hub** | Collect models, prompts, and datasets for the advancement of Ass. | Proposed |

Start with an Ass Card. Label invented examples as illustrative; label observed outputs with their model, prompt, and context so others can inspect them.

## Contribute

Bring a particularly impressive piece of Ass to [Issues](https://github.com/Slow-Research/huggingass/issues): an example, a prompt, or a test that exposes unwarranted agreement. Discuss system behavior and research claims; keep real people out of personal ridicule.

When a research route needs a decision rather than another standing ovation, see [abandon is all you need](https://github.com/Slow-Research/abandon-is-all-you-need).
